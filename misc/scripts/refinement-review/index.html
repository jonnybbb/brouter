What actually changed?

The repairs made the comparison more trustworthy. The current refinement changed only 34 of 460 routes. The other 426 pairs are identical. Better rides for most requests have not been demonstrated.

My expectations, what improved, and what this experiment cannot tell us

I expected the correctness repairs to let the optimizer search and compare real routes again. That happened. I also hoped enough requests would get a useful change within three seconds to justify enabling refinement. That did not happen.

A lower profile cost is the algorithm's criterion. It is not a cyclist happiness score. My expectation for a useful product improvement is a visible benefit on many requests, acceptable response time, and riders choosing the new routes in a blind comparison. This result falls short.
